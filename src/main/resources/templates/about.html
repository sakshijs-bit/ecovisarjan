🌱 About EcoVisarjan

Celebrate with devotion. Immerse with responsibility. Give every idol a meaningful journey beyond visarjan.

Our Purpose

EcoVisarjan is a web-based Ganpati Idol Recovery and Reuse Management System designed to organize and simplify the recovery journey of idols after visarjan.


Devotees can register their idols before visarjan and receive a unique EcoVisarjan Idol ID. The registered idol can then be tracked through collection, storage, transportation, processing, material recovery and reuse.

♻️ Recovery Journey

EcoVisarjan creates a structured journey for recovered idol materials. Each stage helps keep the recovery process organized and transparent.

📝

Registration

Idol details are recorded in the system.

🌊

Visarjan

The idol completes its visarjan ceremony.

🚚

Collection

Registered idols are collected for recovery.

📦

Storage

Collected materials are safely stored.

⚙️

Processing

Recoverable materials are processed.

♻️

Reuse

Recovered materials find a useful second life.

🌍 Our Vision

Our vision is to encourage responsible visarjan practices and promote the recovery and reuse of materials, contributing towards cleaner communities and more sustainable celebrations.

Every idol deserves a second life — not a place in the waste stream. ♻️
